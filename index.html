How quickly can you launch a new vertical or offer?

For most categories, we can move from alignment to first spend within 1–2 weeks, depending on compliance, routing and creative approvals on your side.

Do you handle both lead forms and inbound calls?

Yes. We support lead-only and direct inbound structures. The optimization logic is tuned to your preferred economics.

How do you report performance back to us?

You get access to live dashboards plus a consistent cadence of summary reports and action plans, so your internal team always knows what's happening.

Can you work with our existing tracking and CRM setup?

We integrate with most leading CRMs, call tracking platforms and analytics stacks. During onboarding we map attribution and data flows so nothing breaks.
